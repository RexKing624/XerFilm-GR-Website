## Camera-interface artwork and research references

The 40×40 packaging-style PNGs in `public/films/` were exported from the current custom-firmware film catalog. The original pixel artwork and its provenance must be preserved on redistribution. They are included as existing project assets, not represented as official brand artwork or exclusive original camera assets.

The firmware work referenced by this website builds on open-source research including:

- [Maxwooow/Ricoh-GRMod](https://github.com/Maxwooow/Ricoh-GRMod)
- [DoYitNow/gr-custom-tool](https://github.com/DoYitNow/gr-custom-tool)

This repository contains the website and explicitly approved firmware distribution parts, rather than the complete firmware development projects. Firmware code remains subject to its original licenses; website publication does not change those licenses.

Native Cinema list/processing research also references idontknownihil/gr3-film-mods (MIT, Copyright 2026 idontknownihil). Cinema color resources and icons are extracted Ricoh data, not re-licensed as website artwork. Cinema is an experimental port of fixed effects and is not a calibrated GR IV camera-output match.
