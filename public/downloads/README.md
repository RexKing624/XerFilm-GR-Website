# Same-site firmware downloads

GR IV / HDF V3.0 (v49), GR IIIx / HDF V2.0 (R20), and GR III / HDF V2.0 (R20) are released in ordinary and enhanced variants. These releases have been camera-tested by the user.

GR IIIx V3.0 is withdrawn pending further testing. Ordinary R20 and enhanced R20-H V2.0 downloads are restored. Both are password-free; the existing reading and enhanced-installation acknowledgements remain.

The browser reconstructs each model-specific BIN from 16 MiB parts, verifies every part and the complete SHA-256, then saves fwdc248b.bin, fwdc243b.bin or fwdc239b.bin. Do not flash parts individually.

Compatible official bases stay GR IV 1.11, GR IIIx 1.60 and GR III 2.10. Enhanced installation versions are GR IV 1.12.10.7, GR IIIx 1.61.21.27 and GR III 2.11.21.27.

Pinned unmodified manufacturer backups and LICENSE.txt remain available. The separately labeled historical V1.1 modification source remains unchanged; it is not the corresponding source for current V2.0 / V3.0 downloads.
